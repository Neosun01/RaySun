01 — About

About

I design finance — the flows people don't enjoy but still have to trust. Trading, payments, accounts, onboarding, KYC. Print taught me hierarchy. UI taught me systems. UX is where the two meet — where the surface has to disappear so a user can move money and know exactly what just happened.

Interaction is where I keep landing. In finance especially, the difference between a good screen and a good product is measured in the small moments — the confirmation that lands right, the state that never surprises, the friction placed exactly where it protects the user. My goal on every project is the same: make it feel inevitable.
